# Join / Collaborate

This page is for two audiences: **students** looking for a thesis/capstone/PhD topic, and
**collaborators** (academics, industry partners) considering a joint project. Jump to the
section that applies to you.

## For students

### Research directions I'm looking for students on

* New **thermophysiological models** of human thermal comfort.
* Connecting **data science with human physiology** and the built environment.
* **Occupant-centric control** of building systems.
* Thermal comfort and heat resilience in **vulnerable settings** (e.g. nursing homes).

If your interests overlap with one of these, mention it explicitly when you contact me.

### Funding

Funding options vary by background — e.g. university scholarships, industry-sponsored PhDs, or
your home country's scholarship schemes. Contact me and we can explore what fits your situation.

### Next step

Once you're on board, see [Supervision](./3_supervision.md) for how I run day-to-day supervision.

## For collaborators

### What I bring / who I'm looking for

**What I bring:**
* Human thermal physiology
* Data science and machine learning
* Web development and open-source tooling

**Who I'm looking for:**
* Urban-scale, climate, or building simulation
* HVAC and building control
* Epidemiology
* Human physiology with a clinical/health background
* Policy implementation and translation

If your expertise complements mine, there's likely a good project here — get in touch.

### Funding

Joint grant applications are welcome — national and international schemes both apply depending
on the partners involved (e.g. Australian researchers can now participate in Horizon Europe
alongside domestic schemes such as the NHMRC). Let's discuss what's the best fit once we've
scoped a project together.

### Next step

Read [My README](./1_how_to_work_with_me.md) to see how I like to work and communicate.

## Help maintain our open-source tools

You don't need to start a new project to work with me — contributions to the free, open-source
tools I maintain are always welcome, whether that's a new feature, a bug fix, a documentation
update, or just flagging an issue:

* [pythermalcomfort](https://github.com/CenterForTheBuiltEnvironment/pythermalcomfort) — Python package for thermal comfort indices.
* [jsthermalcomfort](https://github.com/FedericoTartarini/jsthermalcomfort) — JavaScript port of the same models.
* [CBE Thermal Comfort Tool](https://github.com/CenterForTheBuiltEnvironment/comfort_tool)
* [CBE Clima Tool](https://github.com/CenterForTheBuiltEnvironment/clima)
* [Sports Heat Tool](https://github.com/FedericoTartarini/tool-sma-extreme-heat-policy)

Pull requests, issues, and documentation fixes on any of these are genuinely appreciated.
